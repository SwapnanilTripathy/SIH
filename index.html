// why we exist

SkillBridge is one platform,
three worlds in sync

Students graduate with skills nobody's verified. Colleges track outcomes on spreadsheets. Recruiters can't tell real ability from a polished résumé. SkillBridge gives all three a shared source of truth.

Students

A verified skill profile — projects, certifications, and endorsements — that speaks for itself.

Colleges

Live dashboards on placement outcomes and skill gaps across every batch — no end-of-year guesswork.

Recruiters

Search by demonstrated skill, not keyword-stuffed résumés, and reach students before graduation.

// the platform

What SkillBridge actually does

Four pieces, one loop: skills get verified, colleges get visibility, recruiters get signal, and students get found.

01

Verified skill profiles

Every project or certificate a student adds gets peer- or faculty-verified, so a profile actually means something.

02

Placement analytics

Colleges see skill coverage and internship conversion in real time — ahead of placement season, not after it.

03

Skill-first search

Recruiters filter by verified skill and project history instead of digging through stacks of PDFs.

04

Opportunity feed

Internships, hackathons, and campus drives land in a student's feed, matched to their actual profile.

// the loop

Three steps from profile to offer

Each stage is verified before it moves to the next, so nothing on the board is unproven.

01

Build a verified profile

Students add projects, certifications and coursework — the raw material recruiters actually check.

02

Colleges confirm it

Faculty and placement cells sign off on the work before it ever reaches a recruiter's search.

03

Recruiters find you

Recruiters filter by verified skill, not keyword-stuffed résumés, and reach out directly.
